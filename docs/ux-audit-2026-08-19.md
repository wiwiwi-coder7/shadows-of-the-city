# Player Experience Audit — 2026-08-19

## First-session review

The desktop landing page establishes the noir atmosphere, but its primary information and controls occupy a small portion of the viewport. The live save state shows both a generic `CONTINUE` control and a separate `RESUME` control, which makes the next action ambiguous. Secondary destinations compete visually with the return-to-story action and navigation labels are too small at a normal desktop distance.

## In-story review

The player page keeps the scene image and prose legible, but the current-scene loading status remains visible after the artwork is available. Chapter, scene, trace progress, library actions, and control-toggle affordances are fragmented along the top edge. Choice rows have very small numeric markers and limited immediate feedback about what happens to progress after selection.

## Initial improvement direction

Prioritize a single, explicit return-to-story action; a compact, consistently visible story progress rail; more comfortable reading width and type scale; clearer choice hierarchy and active states; and a mobile-first bottom control sheet for non-story destinations. Preserve the noir palette, local saves, keyboard controls, and continuous narrative flow.

## Release verification

The released home page now gives a saved player `RESUME CASE` as the primary action and moves `START OVER` to a deliberately secondary position. The last-trace card remains informative but no longer competes with a duplicate resume button. The player view now presents the current chapter and scene beside a compact trace rail and `SAVED LOCALLY` acknowledgement. Scene art, narration, and choice rows retain high contrast; choices have stronger numbering and decision affordance. The GitHub Pages artifact deployed successfully after the final build.
