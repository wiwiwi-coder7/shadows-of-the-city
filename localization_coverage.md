# Localization Coverage

All public interface labels, controls, prompts, navigation items, and route-level descriptive text are supplied through `LocaleProvider`. The provider contains English strings for the launch experience and complete Persian UI-message coverage with RTL direction support held inactive until the Persian story translation is commissioned.

The ten chapter scripts, codex descriptions, character role descriptions, and scene titles remain authored English narrative content in the approved game data. They are deliberately treated as content assets rather than interface labels. When the Persian narrative edition is prepared, those source records can be translated independently without changing the UI contract or layout direction.
